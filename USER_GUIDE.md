## Managing Dresses

### Adding a New Dress

1. Click **"Dresses"** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **"Add New Dress"** button (top right, gold color)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Name:** The dress name (e.g., "Juliette", "Rose")
   - **Price:** Enter the price (just the number, like 1299)
   - **Description:** Write a detailed description of the dress
   - **Features:** Add special features one at a time:
     - Type a feature (e.g., "Lace sleeves")
     - Click the **Plus (+)** button
     - Repeat for each feature
     - To remove a feature, click the trash icon
   - **New Arrival:** Check this box if it's a new dress
   - **Published:** Check this to make it visible on your website
4. Click **"Save Dress"**

### Uploading Dress Photos

**After creating a dress:**

1. Find the dress in your list
2. Click the **"Images"** button (gold colored)
3. In the popup window:
   - Click **"Choose Files"** or **drag photos** into the dashed box
   - You can upload multiple photos at once
   - Wait for the upload to complete (you'll see a progress bar)
4. **Set a Primary Image:**
   - The primary image shows on the main shop page
   - Click the **star icon** on the photo you want as primary
   - The primary photo will have a gold star badge
5. **Delete Photos:** Click the red **Trash** button on any photo to remove it
6. Click **"Close"** when done

**Photo Tips:**
- Use high-quality photos
- Photos are automatically optimized for web
- Upload at least 3-4 photos of each dress from different angles

### Editing a Dress

1. Go to **Dresses** in the sidebar
2. Find the dress you want to edit
3. Click the **"Edit"** button
4. Make your changes
5. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Deleting a Dress

1. Go to **Dresses** in the sidebar
2. Find the dress you want to remove
3. Click the **red Trash** button
4. Confirm you want to delete it
5. **⚠️ Warning:** This cannot be undone and will delete all photos!

### Publishing/Unpublishing Dresses

- **Published** dresses appear on your website (green badge)
- **Unpublished** dresses are hidden from customers (gray badge)
- Use the **Edit** button to change the published status

### Searching for Dresses

- Use the **search box** at the top to find dresses by name
- Use the **dropdown menu** to filter:
  - All Dresses
  - Published Only
  - Unpublished Only

## Managing Appointments

Click **"Appointments"** in the left sidebar. You'll see four tabs:

### 1. Calendar View

**What You'll See:**
- Color-coded appointments on a calendar
- **Yellow** = Pending (need to confirm)
- **Green** = Confirmed
- **Gray** = Completed
- **Red** = Cancelled

**How to Use:**
1. Click on any appointment to view/edit details
2. Use the arrows to navigate between months
3. Switch between Month, Week, Day, or Agenda views

### 2. List View

**Search and Filter:**
- **Search box:** Type customer name, email, or phone
- **Status filter:** Show only certain statuses
- **Type filter:** Filter by consultation, alterations, or fitting
- **Date range:** Select start and end dates

**Editing an Appointment:**
1. Click the **pencil icon** next to an appointment
2. Update any information needed
3. Change the status:
   - **Pending** → **Confirmed** after calling the customer
   - **Confirmed** → **Completed** after the appointment
   - Any status → **Cancelled** if they cancel
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

**Deleting an Appointment:**
- Click the **red trash icon**
- Confirm the deletion
- **Note:** Consider marking as "Cancelled" instead of deleting to keep records

### 3. Business Hours

**Setting Your Open Hours:**
1. Click the **"Hours"** tab
2. For each day:
   - Click **"Edit"** button (gold)
   - Set your **Open Time** and **Close Time**
   - Or check **"Closed"** for days you're not open
   - Click the **green Save button**

**Example Setup:**
- Monday-Friday: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- Sunday: **Closed** (check the box)

### 4. Blocked Dates

**For Holidays and Vacations:**
1. Click the **"Blocked Dates"** tab
2. Click **"Add Blocked Date"**
3. Select the date
4. Enter a reason (e.g., "Christmas Holiday", "Owner Vacation")
5. Click **"Add"**

**Removing Blocked Dates:**
- Click the **red trash icon** next to the date

## Managing Gallery Images

The gallery images appear on your homepage to showcase your work.

### Adding Gallery Photos

1. Click **"Gallery"** in the left sidebar
2. Click **"Upload Images"** (gold button, top right)
3. Select one or more photos from your computer
4. Wait for upload to complete
5. Photos appear immediately on your homepage

### Adding Captions

1. Find the photo
2. Click the **blue "Edit"** button
3. Type your caption
4. Click the **green "Save"** button

### Reordering Photos

Photos appear in the order you set:
- Click the **up arrow** to move a photo up
- Click the **down arrow** to move a photo down

### Deleting Gallery Photos

1. Click the **red "Delete"** button
2. Confirm deletion

**Photo Tips:**
- Use photos of real brides in your dresses
- Include close-up details
- Add captions mentioning the dress or occasion

## Managing Inquiries

When customers fill out the contact form, their messages appear here.

### Viewing Inquiries

1. Click **"Inquiries"** in the left sidebar
2. Filter by status:
   - **All** - See everything
   - **New** - Unread messages (yellow)
   - **Read** - Messages you've seen (blue)
   - **Handled** - Completed inquiries (green)

### Responding to Inquiries

1. Read the message
2. Click the customer's **email** or **phone** to contact them
3. After responding:
   - Click **"Mark as Read"** (if it's new)
   - Click **"Mark as Handled"** when completely resolved

### Deleting Old Inquiries

- Click the **red "Delete"** button
- Use this for spam or very old messages

**Best Practice:**
- Check inquiries daily
- Respond within 24 hours
- Mark as handled after resolving

## Troubleshooting

### "I forgot my password"

Contact your website administrator (Keaton) to reset it.

### "Photos won't upload"

**Try these steps:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Make sure photos are JPG, PNG, or WebP format
3. Try uploading one photo at a time
4. Try a smaller photo size (under 10MB)
5. Refresh the page and try again

### "Changes aren't showing on my website"

**Try these steps:**
1. **Make sure you clicked "Save"** or "Save Settings"
2. **Refresh your website** (press Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
3. **Clear your browser cache:**
   - Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Cmd+Shift+Delete on Mac)
   - Select "Cached images and files"
   - Click "Clear data"

### "The admin panel is slow"

- Close other browser tabs
- Restart your browser
- Check your internet speed
- Contact support if it persists

### "I made a mistake and need to undo"

- **Deleted a dress by accident:** You'll need to re-create it
- **Wrong appointment info:** Edit it immediately
- **Wrong photo uploaded:** Delete it and upload the correct one

### "Customer can't see an appointment time"

**Check these things:**
1. Is the date blocked in the Blocked Dates section?
2. Are your business hours set correctly for that day?
3. Is there already an appointment at that time?

### "Email confirmations aren't sending"

Contact your website administrator to check the email service configuration.
